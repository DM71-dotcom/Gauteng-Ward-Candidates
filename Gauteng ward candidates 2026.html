Who's on your ward ballot in Gauteng

Every certified ward candidate in all nine Gauteng municipalities for the 4 November 2026 local government elections, from the IEC's final candidate list.

Type your ward number, or the 8-digit ward ID (it picks the municipality for you) the IEC sends when you SMS your ID number to 32810. Not sure? Look up your ward on the IEC site.
